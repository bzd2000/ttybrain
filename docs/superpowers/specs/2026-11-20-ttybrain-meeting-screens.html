Meeting screens for ttybrain

Proposed screens for the reworked meetings: every meeting belongs to a person or a project (the board is a project too), meetings can recur, each person and project keeps a talk-list of things to discuss, and actions become their own plugin that links to anything. Same frame and style as the first screen designs. All names, projects and dates are invented; "today" is Friday 20 Nov 2026.

Every person, project and meeting gets an Actions tab from the actions plugin, listing the actions linked to it.

Your existing actions move over with their A-n numbers; their project, owner, "for" person and meeting become links.

Decided on these screens

  1. Talk items tagged with a project also show at that project's next meeting, until they are discussed in either place.
  2. No time of day: meetings have a date only. Upcoming lists sort by date, then by series.
  3. Stakeholders have talk-lists too, with or without a recurring meeting.
  4. Meetings close themselves 3 days after their date, keeping notes and carrying unticked talk items forward.
  5. Decisions stay with projects (in the projects plugin). A decision taken in a meeting still records which meeting.
